Who we are

Shivalya Agrotech Producer Company Limited is a private limited company based in Ambala City, Haryana, registered under GST since 17 July 2020.

We supply UV stabilized mulching films, jute thread, agricultural plastic rolls, pre-cut mulch sheets, yarn cones and custom films to farmers, manufacturers and businesses across India.

Our aim is simple: dependable material, fair price and on-time delivery, so farmers can grow more with less effort.
